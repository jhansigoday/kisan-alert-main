Calculation Formulas

  • Total Cost: (Seed + Fertilizer + Pesticide + Labour + Irrigation + Machinery + Other) × Land Area
  • Expected Yield: Baseline Yield × Seasonal Factor × Soil Match × Water Match
  • Expected Revenue: Total Yield × Mandi Modal Price
  • Net Profit: Expected Revenue − Total Cost
  • Profit Margin: (Net Profit / Expected Revenue) × 100

Uncertainty Model

Estimated ranges are computed deterministically by applying a +/-10% yield variance, +/-5% market price variance, and +/-5% input cost variance to simulate variable seasonal conditions.

* Actual returns may vary depending on weather, yield, input costs, and market prices.
